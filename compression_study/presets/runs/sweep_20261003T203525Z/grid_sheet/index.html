What this shows

  • Whole-crop views: 1600×900, 2304×1296 and 3072×1728 at every budget that fits, downscaled inline and full resolution in full screen. Zoomed views: the sliders show 1600×900, 2304×1296 and the largest Zero-feasible / passing ROI per budget (180: 1600×900, 2304×1296; 250: 1600×900, 2304×1296, 3072×1728; 500: 1600×900, 2304×1296, 3072×1728). Every cell's numbers are in the table.
  • Slider: left = RAW reference, right = nrjxl decoded back to raw; both rendered by one function (bilinear demosaic, the camera's locked WB gains, clipped at sensor white, the RAW's own card matrix, sRGB). Nothing is upsampled on this sheet.
  • Distances come from the Step C byte-target search (≤ 3 encodes, no quality floor here), with an exact bisection when the search ends without a fit below d 15.
  • ΔE reliability: 10 of 13 card colour patches usable (glare / noise); RAW ΔE varies 1.17–1.42 across identical frames, so ΔE differences under ~0.3 are noise. Tag acutance uses 2 tags of ~32 px.
  • Zero cost is measured on frame 0 at frame 0's distance (encode time barely depends on distance). Full frame: the production guard stops cjxl (rfb=mem).
  • The 195-message point from Step A sits in levers.csv (not repeated here).
Data: compression_study/presets/runs/sweep_20261003T203525Z (grid.csv, grid_pjpg.csv, pi_grid.txt). Encoder: bm_cam_legacy #120 (bd6f38c); identical bytes on Mac and Pi.
